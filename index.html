About

Nimbrella blends “nimbus” (rain cloud) and “umbrella” to show exactly what the app does: it tells you when to grab your umbrella. Playful, easy to say, and unique among weather apps.

Nimbrella WeatherApp is a small weather app built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It reads live data from a public weather API and keeps everything else out of the way.
